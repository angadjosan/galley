Refund policy
=============

This document uses _underscore emphasis_ and __underscore strong__ throughout.

Eligibility
-----------

+ Purchased within 30 days
+ Unused and in original packaging
+ Accompanied by a receipt

Exceptions
----------

1) Digital goods are non-refundable.
2) Gift cards are non-refundable.

    return order.total
