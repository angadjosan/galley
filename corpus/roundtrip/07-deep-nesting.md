# Deep nesting

- level one
  - level two
    - level three
      - level four
        - level five

A second list that uses a different marker:

* star one
* star two
  + plus nested
  + plus nested again

An ordered list that starts at zero:

0. zero
1. one
2. two

An ordered list with a paren delimiter and a wide start:

7) seven
8) eight

Task list:

- [ ] not done yet
- [x] already done
- [ ] nested parent
  - [x] nested child

Empty list items are legal:

-
- second item after an empty one
-

Blockquotes three deep:

> level one of the quote
>
> > level two of the quote
> >
> > > level three of the quote

A quote containing a list containing a quote:

> - a bullet inside a quote
>   - a nested bullet
>
>   > a quote inside a list inside a quote

A loose list, whose items are separated by blank lines:

- loose one

- loose two

A tight list immediately after a paragraph:
- tight one
- tight two
